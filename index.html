Exact PRN recording times

When display-time rounding is enabled, the public history shows the rounded time. The original recorded timestamp is shown here for Pharmacist review.

A backup contains the medication list and dose history. Store it securely and do not share it publicly. Importing replaces the current local data. Resetting removes all medications and history from this browser.

Prototype safety note: MedTrack saves locally in this browser and can sync shared app data to Supabase after an authorized administrator signs in. Sync availability depends on network access, account permissions, and Supabase configuration; this prototype does not send dependable reminders. Browser data may be lost if site data is cleared, so export secure backups regularly. Follow the prescription label and clinician/pharmacist instructions. Interval mode calculates a suggested next time from a recorded dose, but must only be used when that matches the prescribed instructions. PRN (as-needed) medications can be logged manually and are not scheduled by the app. Optional PRN limits can warn about a configured maximum in a rolling 24 hours or minimum interval; they are not a substitute for prescription instructions and do not decide when or how much medication to take.
